    Create your sprint

    Upload notes or existing questions and choose up to 3 interests. They are sent together to create up to 50 new questions. Interests personalise Theory questions; Practical questions apply the uploaded material. Set the count and question type on your study desk. Mixed uses 60% practical and 40% theory.

    Add up to 10 sources in total: files and public website links. Files can be PDF, Word (.docx), TXT, Markdown, or JSON, up to 8 MB combined. Word body text is read; images are not extracted. Save older .doc files as .docx or PDF first.

    Files and links stay in this browser session until you request questions. They are then read and sent securely to Google AI through the study service. Websites must be publicly readable over HTTPS; sign-in pages and pages that require JavaScript may need to be saved as PDF instead.
