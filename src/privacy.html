Privacy and uploads

Updated 28 October 2026. Studioza is an anonymous study preview maintained by the Studioza project maintainer. No account is required.

Choose suitable material

Use synthetic or non-sensitive material you have permission to upload. Do not upload student records, private assessments, confidential research, credentials, financial, health or other sensitive personal information. Remove unnecessary names and personal details first. PDF files are sent in full, including any embedded metadata; Word and text files are converted to text.

What is sent

When you create a sprint, your selected document contents, filenames, interests and question settings go to Studioza's Cloudflare Worker and Google Gemini to produce questions. Cloudflare Turnstile processes a security check. Cloudflare provides hosting, transport and usage controls. Website imports are temporarily unavailable.

What stays on this device

Generated questions, source labels, answers, progress, selected interests, course name and accessibility preferences are saved in this browser profile. Raw selected files stay in tab memory and are not restored after a reload. Saved study data expires after 30 days without study activity when you next open Studioza. On a shared device, other people using the same browser profile may see saved study data.

Clear your data

Study menu → Clear upload history removes uploaded selections and generated questions with their related progress; it keeps interests and preferences. Clear all saved study data removes Studioza's questions, progress, interests and preferences from this browser. You can also clear Studioza site data in browser settings. Device-local deletion does not delete information already processed by Google or Cloudflare.

Operational logs and providers

The application emits request reference IDs, release version, event type, status and whether AI processing began. Application logs do not intentionally include document contents, filenames, interests, security tokens or IP addresses. Cloudflare and Google may process connection/security metadata under their own service arrangements. Provider retention, international-transfer and model-training settings must be confirmed by the operator before a student pilot; this preview does not promise zero provider retention or claim compliance approval.

Third-party assets

Application styles and icons are self-hosted. Google Fonts serves typography assets; Cloudflare serves the Turnstile security widget. No advertising or application analytics tracker is intentionally added.

Support and privacy questions

For non-sensitive support, contact the project maintainer using Studioza's issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include uploaded documents, personal information, tokens or credentials. A named operator and private privacy-contact route are still required before inviting students to a pilot.

Pilot status

Independent security and data-protection review is pending. This page describes the implementation; it is not a lawful-basis, DPIA, data-transfer or contractual decision. Personal-data processing for a student pilot must not start until those decisions and the published operator/contact details are complete.
