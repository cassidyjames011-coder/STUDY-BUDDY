    Document Upload

    Upload your course notes or slides to create 100 questions: 60 practical and 40 theory. Your selected interests help personalise the examples.

    Choose up to 10 PDF, TXT, or Markdown files, with a combined size of 8 MB.

    Files stay in this browser session until you request questions. They are then sent securely to Google AI through the study service to create your sprint.

    Personalise questions

    Search for an interest and select it to add it instantly. Choose up to 3. To use your own topic, type it and select the matching suggestion, or press Enter. Remove a topic with its × button.

    Your selected topics are saved on this device and included with your documents when you create questions. They help shape memorable examples while keeping the course concepts accurate.

    Personalise theory updates your existing theory questions. Practise weak spots creates 10 questions based on missed or flagged topics. Both require a selected interest.

    Security verification runs when you request AI questions. Your existing questions stay available if generation fails.
